  willpower to train — not going would be the punishment. You have to "become NOT you."

  effect. The two great sins are dodging responsibility and living in denial.

  staying the same (numb then explodes into regret). Effort warps time.

  lightning bolt into a lightbulb; it explodes every time" unless you've become the person who
  can hold it. Having everything is torture; chasing is fulfillment.
