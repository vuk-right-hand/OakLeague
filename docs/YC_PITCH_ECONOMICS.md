## Part 2: Unit Economics

### 2.1 Unit Costs Per Minute (PAYG Rates, April 2026)

**Talk session — per minute composite:**

| Component | Cost/min | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gemini 3.1 Flash Live (brain/STT) | $0.011 | Audio in ($3/1M tokens) + audio out ($12/1M tokens) at 25 tokens/sec; 50/50 user/AI speaking split |
| Cartesia Sonic 3 (cloned TTS) | $0.015 | ~75 words of AI output per 30s of speech; developer tier estimate |
| Simli Trinity-1 (avatar lipsync) | $0.004 | Announced <$0.01/min; billed only when AI is speaking (~50% of session) so effective is ~$0.004/session-min |
| LiveKit Cloud (WebRTC transport) | $0.005 | Agent session + audio egress |
| **Total talk COGS** | **$0.035–$0.040** | Use $0.040 for conservative modeling; $0.025 at committed-use volume |

**Feed (passive video) — per delivered minute:**

| Provider | Cost/min | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mux PAYG | $0.0008 | Per minute of video delivered; pre-buffered HLS segments count |
| Mux enterprise | $0.0005–0.0006 | 20–30% off list at volume |
| Cloudflare R2 + CDN | ~$0.000 | Zero egress fees; pay storage only ($0.015/GB/month) |

**HLS buffering reality:** With 10s pre-buffer and average swipe at 15–20s per clip, actual video delivered per 60-min session ≈ **35–40 minutes** (not 60). Effective Mux PAYG feed cost: ~$0.028–0.032/session/day = **~$0.80/user/month** (not $1.80 as a naive 60-min calculation would suggest).

**At Cloudflare R2 path (1M+ users):** Feed cost drops to **$0.05/user/month**. Video library stored in R2, served via Cloudflare CDN with zero egress. You still use Mux for encoding (one-time $0.015/min per video), then push assets to R2.

### 2.2 Creator Video Library — Storage Cost

Short videos = 60 sec · Long videos = 11 min average

| Scale | Short videos | Long videos | Total stored minutes | Monthly storage (Mux) | One-time encoding |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 creators | 100 × 50 × 1 min = 5,000 min | None | 5,000 min | **$12/mo** | $75 |
| 10K creators | 10K × 50 × 1 min = 500K min | 10K × 10 × 11 min = 1.1M min | 1.6M min | **$3,840/mo** | $24,000 |

Library cost is noise at user scale — at 1M users, $3,840/mo in library storage vs $2M+ in user COGS.

At R2 path: 1.6M min ≈ 1.27 TB → $0.015/GB × 1,270 GB = **$19/month storage regardless of views.**
